About this place

The cabin is not a real place: it is drawn for this site, with nobody in it. The hour, the season and the weather come from your device’s own clock, and nothing about you is sent anywhere. Version 4.1.050.

What the room is made of

The materials, furniture and photographs in this room are free works, kept with the site and credited here. The logs, the roof, the stone, the stair, the loft, the curtains, the fire and the clock’s hands are drawn for the scene. The sound is made on your device, from no recording.
