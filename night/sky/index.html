    About this sky

    Where you are
    On the deck of a cabin in the Rocky Mountains, at night. The peaks are Longs Peak and Mount Meeker.

    Nothing about you is sent anywhere: the place and the time are worked out on this device, and the sound is made or played on it. Version 4.1.050.

    Credits for the deck and its sky
