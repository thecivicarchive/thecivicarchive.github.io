My ballot

A ballot you kept on this device. It stays here, opens with no signal, and is never sent anywhere.

Anyone holding your unlocked phone can see which districts this ballot is for.

Keep it on your Home Screen

  • iPhone: in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: in Chrome, tap the menu (⋮), then Add to Home screen or Install.

It opens as “My ballot” and works with no signal.

On an iPhone, save a PDF as well. If you use Safari for seven days without opening this site, Safari can clear what the site keeps. So a ballot kept today may be gone by Election Day. A Home Screen copy keeps its own storage, so it may open empty. If it does, open your state’s ballot page from it, put your ballot together, and tap “Keep this ballot on my phone” there. The surest copy is a PDF. Tap Print or save as PDF, then Share, then Save to Files. It stays until you delete it.

“Forget this ballot” removes it from this device, and so does “Forget my location and choices” on your state’s ballot page. The ballot is a preview from the official candidate lists, not an official ballot: your county’s sample ballot is the authority.
