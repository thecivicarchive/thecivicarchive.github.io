25 states and the District of Columbia publish a live count this site reads as it comes in, and seven more can be read with care. Minnesota's figures come from the Secretary of State's results files, saved through the night, and Oklahoma's from the files its election office posts for export, saved by hand. For the other 16, the page links to the state's own results and adds the official totals when the state certifies them. The rules this space keeps

How Election Night works

Every page of Election Night keeps these rules, on the night and after it.

Every page here keeps these rules.

  • Official counts only.

    Every result comes from the state's own election office, with the time the state gave it, and says it is not final until the state certifies it.

    Every number comes from the state. Each one shows when it was posted.

  • No race is called.

    A candidate is ahead in the count so far until the result is certified. The site never says projected, called or won before then.

    We never say who won until the state says so.

  • Forecasts are Analysis.

    A forecast is a model's estimate with a range, its inputs and its track record, and it is labelled Analysis everywhere it appears.

    A forecast is a guess. It is always marked as one.

  • Headlines, not articles.

    The feed shows a headline, the outlet, the time and a link. It never copies a story.

    We show news headlines with links. We do not copy stories.

  • People are counted, not shown.

    Posts are shown only from news organizations, election offices and candidates' official accounts. Everyone else is counted, never shown or named.

    We never show or name regular people. We only count their posts.

  • Red and blue mean party.

    Red and blue are used for party data and for nothing else. Everything else is brass and green.

    Red and blue only ever mean a party.

Plain answers

Where do the numbers come from?

Only from each state's own election office, as it posts them, each with its time. Minnesota's come from the Secretary of State's results files. Where a state publishes no live count this site may read, the page links to the state's own results and adds the official totals when the state certifies them.

Does this site call races?

No. A candidate is “ahead in the count so far” until the state certifies the result. Forecasts are a computer model's estimate, labelled Analysis, with a range and a track record. The official count decides.

How fresh are the figures?

The pages look for new figures every few minutes. A figure usually reaches this site 10 to 20 minutes after the state posts it, and every number carries the time the state gave it.

Does this site track me?

No page here counts visits. Times are shown in your own time zone, worked out on your device. Where a page offers “Use my location”, your place is worked out on your device and is not sent anywhere.
