Minnesota's counties, closer to home.

A county board sets the levy, runs the roads, the jail, the courthouse and the human services office. Every county office in Minnesota is elected on a nonpartisan ballot. This page shows who holds each one, as the Secretary of State's official election results record it, and nothing that is not in that record.

Who holds each office

The Minnesota Secretary of State's official election results: the winner of each county office on the general-election ballot, with the votes. No election has been read yet. A resignation or an appointment since an election is not in this record; the county's own site is the authority for who holds an office today.

Party

Every county office in Minnesota is nonpartisan on the ballot, so no party is shown, and none is guessed.
