What was changed here (2026-10-03, The Civic Archive): the ASCII FBX 6.1 mesh was converted to a glTF 2.0 binary with
the same 3,088 vertices and 6,069 triangles; it was turned into a frame of its own (coiling axis +Y, apex up, the
aperture facing +Z and down, the origin on the axis) and stretched 1.175x along the coiling axis to a garden snail's
proportions; the scan's colour map was left out (the companion paints its colour in a shader) and the 4096 px normal
and occlusion/roughness/metallic maps were shrunk to 1024 px and 512 px and re-encoded as JPEG; the aperture's and the
apex's positions were written into the file's extras. No other change. The original download's SHA-256 is
544e647c39d761f3b9601530ca65557da2c67e7c6e0f6d4a4b708881db768cbd; this file's is
a2562310cf287b9cd6151bd41299bad83d96f7c2930e945c70b72c206081bb19.
