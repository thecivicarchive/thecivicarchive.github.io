# County and local races on the ballot: where each state stands

Updated 2026-10-01, after waves one and two. Saved version: v4.0.075. Minnesota is loaded in full (4,395 county and local races).

This file is kept by Claude during the build. The first part is the list of things only your own browser can get;
the second is the state-by-state picture from the scouting pass (2026-09-30) and what has been loaded since.

## Waiting on you

Save each file into the folder named (make the folder if it is not there). Never mind the ones marked optional.
The loaders read whatever is there the next time they run; nothing else is needed from you.

## What the scouts said about elections held at another time

- **Alabama**: Mayors, councils, city school boards: municipal elections, August 2025 or August 25, 2026 (Secretary's calendars; Code 11-46-21 not read at source). Probate judges, circuit clerks, district attorneys, constables: none printed, other years. No townships or special-district boards.
- **Alaska**: Most cities and boroughs vote October 6 (AS 29.26.040: first Tuesday of October unless local ordinance differs), including Fairbanks North Star, Juneau and Mat-Su's cities. Anchorage votes in April (next 2027). State-run REAA school boards: October 6 (AS 14.08.071).
- **Arizona**: Sheriff, county attorney, recorder, treasurer, assessor, school superintendent and supervisors: 2028. City seats won outright at the July 21 primary. Tucson city offices: odd years. Uncontested school and district elections are cancelled (A.R.S. 16-410). Precinct committeemen: primary.
- **Arkansas**: School boards: March 3, 2026, with the primary (Act 503 of 2025); only runoffs in November. Judges and prosecutors: nonpartisan general election March 3; runoffs November. County and city runoffs: December 1, 2026. Conservation and improvement districts: not verified.
- **California**: County offices and judges won outright on June 2 (Elections Code 1300, 8140); most sheriffs and district attorneys (2028); cities and districts still voting in odd years (Code 1301-1303); uncontested seats, often filled by appointment instead.
- **Colorado**: School boards (November of odd years; next November 2, 2027). Special district boards (May of odd years, C.R.S. 1-13.5-111). Most towns (April, even years) and cities (November, odd years). Denver's regular offices (2027). District attorneys (not on the 2026 list). No townships.
- **Connecticut**: Regular municipal elections (mayors, selectmen, councils, most school boards) are in odd years, November or May (CGS 9-164). Special districts elect at their own annual meetings (CGS 7-327). Justices of the peace are party-nominated, never on a ballot.
- **Delaware**: School boards: second Tuesday in May (May 12, 2026). Cities and towns: each on its own charter date, none on November 3 (Department's 2026 municipal calendar). Wilmington city offices and other county seats: 2028. Judges are appointed. No townships.
- **Florida**: Sheriff, clerk, property appraiser, tax collector, superintendent: 2028 (s. 100.041). School board and judge races settled on August 18, and unopposed candidates, are not printed (s. 105.051). Many cities vote on charter dates (Broward: March 10, 2026).
- **Georgia**: Judges, nonpartisan school boards and consolidated governments were decided May 19, 2026 (runoff June 16). Sheriffs, court clerks, tax commissioners and probate judges are elected in presidential years. Regular city elections are in odd years (last: November 4, 2025).
- **Hawaii**: Eight council seats were won outright on August 8 (Hawaii County 1, 2, 3, 7, 8, 9; Honolulu II, VIII). In 2028: Honolulu and Hawaii County mayors and prosecutors, Kauai prosecutor, Honolulu council I, III, V, VII, IX.
- **Idaho**: Cities and school boards (November, odd years). Fire, cemetery, recreation and, since a 2026 law (H873), soil and water districts (November, odd years). Highway, hospital, water and sewer districts (May, odd years); library districts (odd years).
- **Illinois**: Cities, villages, townships, school boards (except Chicago's), park, library, fire and community college districts: consolidated election, April of odd years (next April 2027). Peoria's school board and party committeepersons: March primary. State's attorney, circuit clerk, coroner: not on 2026's list.
- **Indiana**: City offices (mayor, council, clerk, city judge) and most town offices: municipal election, November 2027. Marion County township boards: 2028. County offices not due this cycle (varies by county): 2028. No special district office appears in the Division's guide or lists.
- **Iowa**: Mayors, city councils, school boards and community college boards: November of odd years (held November 4, 2025; next 2027; Iowa Code 376.1, 277.1, 260C.15). County auditor and sheriff: 2028 (Code 39.17). Sanitary district trustees: special elections (Code 358.9).
- **Kansas**: Cities, school boards, community colleges and drainage, extension, irrigation, improvement, water and hospital districts vote in November of odd years (K.S.A. 25-21a01; next 2027). County clerk, treasurer, register of deeds, attorney, sheriff, township trustee and treasurer: 2028.
- **Kentucky**: Governor and statewide officers (2027). Full terms of circuit judges, circuit clerks, Commonwealth's attorneys (2030). Other mayors, school board seats, even Louisville council districts (2028). Fire district trustees vote in June (KRS 75.031). No townships or spring elections.
- **Louisiana**: Sheriffs, clerks of court, assessors, coroners, police juries and most parish councils (October 2027, with the Governor); Orleans city and parish offices (fall 2025); Orleans school board and other towns (2028); spring-cycle towns (May 16 and June 27, 2026).
