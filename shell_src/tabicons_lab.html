Tab icons lab

The live 3D icons of a member page's tabs, drawn into a mock rail exactly as the page mounts them. From the console: __lab.still(w, h) returns the rail as a PNG data URL; __lab.sheet() every icon at rest, hovered, focused, chosen and still, light and dark; __lab.bench(n) the cost of a frame; __lab.shot(name, dataURL) saves through the local saver on port 8799. Nothing here is linked or published.
