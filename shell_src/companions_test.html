Companions side by side

Seven companions, drawn in code. Each one is shown at its real size in the corner of a page, and at three times that size. Hover over one, or move to it with the keyboard, and it dances. A click plays its greeting. The buttons play each clip.

This is a draft page for judging them. Nothing here is sent anywhere.
